What the end product is

A workspace memory platform for AI-native companies. It ingests team activity, extracts decisions, facts, tasks, and relationships, then serves that context back through search, briefings, and agent-ready APIs.

Capture

Connect transcripts, chats, docs, and CRM records. The system detects what matters instead of asking employees to maintain a wiki by hand.

Structure

Convert raw activity into memory objects linked to clients, projects, people, and open questions, with citations and time context.

Activate

Inject the right context into internal copilots, support tools, meeting prep, onboarding flows, and daily decision-making.

How it works

The product sits between the company’s operational data and the humans or agents who need reliable context.

Ingest team activity

Meetings, voice notes, chats, notes, CRM updates, and internal docs enter one unified memory pipeline.

Extract memory signals

The platform identifies decisions, promises, facts, tasks, preferences, blockers, and account-specific context.

Build memory graph

Each memory item is linked to people, accounts, projects, meetings, and source evidence with timestamps and permissions.

Return context on demand

Users and AI agents receive grounded answers, timelines, and briefings with a clear trace back to source material.

Why teams pay for it

Most teams do not have a knowledge problem. They have a memory problem. Context exists, but it is scattered, stale, and unavailable at the moment of action.

Faster onboarding

New hires stop asking the same questions because the company’s operating memory is searchable and current.

Better AI outputs

Internal assistants answer with company-specific context instead of generic guesses.

Less dropped context

Teams keep track of prior commitments, client preferences, and decision history across channels.

Not another knowledge base. A memory system that works at the speed of the team.
